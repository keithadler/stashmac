Show HN: Stash for Mac – encrypted backup into the free storage you already have
https://github.com/keithadler/stashmac

Most people have 15 GB of Google Drive, 5 GB of OneDrive or iCloud, and no backup of the
folder that matters. Stash for Mac backs up the folders you choose into any of those (they all
mount as folders through their own apps, so no API keys, no accounts), encrypted on the Mac
first with ChaChaPoly. The provider only ever sees blobs with keyed-hash names.

No password: the app makes a random key and shows it once as 24 words plus a QR code on a
printable card. Checksum catches typos; a photo of the card restores on a new Mac. The app
never keeps a copy, so a lost card means an unreadable backup, and it says so up front.

The part I cared about: it verifies itself. Every week it restores a random file to a temp
folder to prove the whole path works. Old snapshots age out and unreferenced pieces are
deleted so the free tier stays free. Never downloads cloud placeholders behind your back.

Swift, CryptoKit, no dependencies, no Xcode project. MIT. macOS 14+.
